About TrueCostToLive

A channel about one question: what does it really cost to live there?

The channel

TrueCostToLive publishes long-form episodes and Shorts on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok. Each episode takes one US city and works out the real cost of living there: the typical home, the monthly payment at today's rate, the salary a lender wants, what that salary keeps after taxes, and which local jobs can afford it alone or as a couple.

The goal is to make a move or a first home concrete. Instead of rankings and rules of thumb, you see the actual numbers, and what changes with a lower rate, a smaller home, or a second income.

How episodes are made

The creator writes the methodology and reviews the numbers. Scripts are generated from public data and a housing-cost model, narrated with the creator's own voice using voice-cloning software, and illustrated with AI-generated artwork and data charts. All data sources are cited in each video's description. See how we calculate.

The TrueCostToLive app

TrueCostToLive also has a small desktop app, used only by the channel's creator, that publishes the channel's finished videos to the creator's own accounts. It is not offered to other users.

What it does

  • Uploads finished videos, titles, descriptions, captions and thumbnails to the TrueCostToLive YouTube channel (YouTube Data API).
  • Posts the same videos to the creator's own TikTok account (TikTok Login Kit and Content Posting API).
  • Publishes Reels to the creator's own Instagram and Facebook accounts (Meta APIs).
  • Reads the channel's own statistics to see which videos perform best (YouTube Analytics API).

Sign-in and data

The app signs in with each platform's own login page (OAuth 2.0), only for the creator's accounts. Sign-in tokens and statistics are stored only on the creator's computer and are never sold or shared. Details are in the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
