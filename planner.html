Two versions: the free Quick Check takes four numbers; the Full Plan ($9.99) takes everything.

Questions

How do I get it?

On our Beacons page: the Quick Check is free (just your email), the Full Plan is $9.99. Either way you get a short PDF with a "Make my copy" button for Google Sheets, plus the Excel file, by email. Your copy is private to you.

Do I need Excel?

No. A free Google account works. Excel works too.

Where do the numbers come from?

US Census ACS (home values, rent, incomes, property tax), BLS wage data, BEA price levels, FHFA home prices, the Freddie Mac mortgage rate and IRS 2026 tax tables. Every assumption is on its own tab, and you can change any of them.

Is this financial advice?

No. It's an educational calculator. Taxes are simplified and returns are assumptions, not promises. Check big decisions with a qualified professional.
